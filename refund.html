Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy applies to payments made to SSPART Enterprise Private Limited for our products and services. It is designed to be fair and simple. If anything is unclear, write to accounts@sspart-enterprise.com before you pay.

1. Product subscriptions (e.g. Atlas Team, DheQuest, Synvord plans)

  • Cancel anytime from your account settings or by emailing support@sspart-enterprise.com. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep access until then.
  • Monthly plans: the current month is not refunded once the billing period has started.
  • Annual plans: if you cancel within 30 days of the start or renewal date, we refund the unused full months on a pro-rata basis. After 30 days, the plan runs to the end of the year and is not refunded.
  • Free plans and trials never charge you; no refund applies.

2. Custom software and services

  • Advance payments are refundable only for work that has not started. Once work has started, the advance is adjusted against the work done.
  • Milestone payments for milestones not delivered are refundable on a pro-rata basis if the engagement is cancelled; delivered and accepted milestones are not refundable.
  • If you cancel an engagement, you pay for work done up to the cancellation date and any committed third-party costs; we refund the balance of any amount paid in advance.
  • If we cancel or cannot deliver, we refund all amounts paid for undelivered work.
  • The detailed terms in your signed proposal, Statement of Work or onboarding terms prevail.

3. Payment errors

Duplicate payments, payments charged in error, and failed transactions where money was debited but no order or subscription was created are refunded in full.

4. How to request a refund

Email accounts@sspart-enterprise.com with your invoice or transaction number, the payment date and the reason. We confirm eligibility within 3 working days.

5. When you get your money

Approved refunds are processed within 14 working days to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or bank transfer). Your bank or card issuer may take a further 5–7 working days to show the credit. We do not charge a fee for refunds.
