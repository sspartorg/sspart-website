Questions

Can I run several hotels?

Yes. One account covers all your properties, and each property keeps its own logins, branding and reports.

Do guests need an app?

No. They scan a QR code in the room or open a link, then sign in with a phone code or their stay code.

Does it work with smart door locks?

Not at launch. Keys are issued by hand and recorded in the system.

Can it read guest IDs automatically?

Not at launch. It captures ID photos and stores them securely; automatic reading is planned for later.

Which countries are supported?

India, UAE and Singapore, each with its own tax invoice and guest registration format.

When will Synvord PMS be available?

It is being built now. Ask for early access and we will tell you as soon as it opens.
