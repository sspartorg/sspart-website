Questions

Which channels can I use at launch?

Email, WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram and in-app push. If one channel fails to deliver, the next one in your fallback order takes over.

How do you handle consent and data protection?

No message is sent without a current consent record for that person, channel and purpose. Rules for India's DPDP Act, the EU GDPR, Singapore's PDPA and the UAE PDPL are built in, and a withdrawal applies across every channel within an hour.

Will AI send messages on its own?

No. AI drafts and recommends, and a person in a role you choose approves every send, journey and agent.

Can I measure print and events?

Yes. Generate QR codes and unique codes for print, hoardings and events, and staff can log the sales that quote them.

Can I use my own email provider?

Yes. Bring your own SES, SendGrid or SMTP servers and sending domains; the DNS set-up is guided in the product.
