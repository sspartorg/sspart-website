Questions

Does DheQuest hold my money?

No. DheQuest connects to your existing broker accounts and places orders through them. Your funds stay with your broker at all times.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new organisation gets a 14-day trial of the Basic plan, with no card needed, including one strategy so you can try the full flow.

What plans are there?

Basic (1 user), Premium (3 users) and Business (10 users), billed monthly in advance in rupees. Current prices are on the Plans page in DheQuest, and GST, where it applies, is shown at checkout.

Which brokers are supported?

AngelOne, Fyers, Zerodha, Groww, Upstox, Kotak Neo, Axis Direct and Dhan for Indian equity and F&O, and Binance, Delta Exchange and CoinDCX for crypto.

Can I test a strategy without real money?

Yes. Back-test it against history, including slippage and costs, then run it on paper trading. We strongly recommend paper trading before going live.

How do I get alerts?

Through Telegram, or browser notifications. There are no email alerts.
