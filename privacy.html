Privacy policy

SSPART collects personal information through this website only when you send us an enquiry. The form asks for your name, mobile number and message, and optionally your email and company. We use these details to reply to you and to keep a record of our conversation, and we do not sell them.

Enquiries are stored with Supabase, our database provider, and only SSPART staff can read them. We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it for that conversation. To see, correct or delete yours, contact us using the address below.

If you email or call us instead, we use what you share only to reply and to provide the service you asked about. This site loads its icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare); like any web request, that service receives your browser's IP address and basic request details.

For questions about our privacy practices, please contact us at contact@sspart-enterprise.com.
