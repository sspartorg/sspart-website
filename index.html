How an engagement runs

A steady rhythm of working releases, so you see progress in the product rather than in status reports.

  1. Discover

    We sit with the people who will use the software and map how the work actually happens today.

    • Process map
    • Scope and estimate
    • Risk register

  2. Design

    Screens, data model and architecture are agreed before the build starts, with a clickable prototype.

    • Clickable prototype
    • Architecture decision log
    • Release plan

  3. Build

    Each release is tested, reviewed and deployed to a staging environment you can use.

    • Working software every release
    • Automated tests
    • Release notes

  4. Run

    We operate, monitor and improve what we shipped, or hand it over with full documentation.

    • Monitoring and alerts
    • Support plan
    • Handover documentation

Engineering standards we hold ourselves to

The same rules apply to our own products and to every client engagement.

You own what we build

Source code, data and intellectual property from custom work belong to you, in your repositories from day one.

Your data, your infrastructure

Questions clients ask us

Anything else, ask us directly.

Do you only sell your own products?

No. We license and customise our products, and we also build custom software from scratch. Many engagements combine both: a product as the base, with custom work on top.

Can a product be branded and customised for us?

Yes. Neel ships with four themes and a store profile you control, and every product can be extended with your own workflows, reports and integrations.

Where is our data hosted?

It depends on the product. Custom work runs in your own cloud account; Atlas and Neel can be self-hosted; DheQuest is a hosted service. We can also host and operate custom work for you.

Who owns the code for custom work?

You do. Source code, data and documentation from custom engagements are yours and live in your repositories.

What happens after go-live?

We offer support plans with monitoring, fixes and planned improvements, or a full handover to your team with documentation and training.

How is an engagement priced?

Fixed scope and fixed price for well-defined work, or a dedicated monthly team for products that keep evolving. Product pricing depends on the product and how widely you use it, and we share it on a demo call.
