Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how SSPART Enterprise Private Limited ("SSPART", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit sspart-enterprise.com, contact us, or use our products and services (Atlas, DheQuest, Synvord and custom software we build). We act as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") for the data described here. When we process data on a client's behalf inside software we build or run for them, we act as their Data Processor and follow their instructions and our contract with them.

1. What we collect

When What Why
You send an enquiry, email or call Name, work email, phone, company, your message, the product you asked about To reply and to provide the demo, proposal or service you asked for
You use a product account Name, email, phone, organisation, login and usage data, content you add To provide the product, secure your account, give support and bill you
You pay us Billing name, address, amount, transaction ID. Card/UPI details are handled by our payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay); we never see or store full card numbers To process payment, issue invoices and meet tax and accounting law
You visit the website IP address and basic request data (sent by your browser to our hosting and icon providers). Analytics data only if you accept the analytics banner To keep the site running and secure, and (with consent) to understand which pages help visitors
You apply for a job or internship CV, contact details, education and work history To assess your application

We do not collect sensitive data such as health, biometric or financial account data through the website, and we do not knowingly collect data of children under 18. Our products are meant for adults and businesses. Where a product is used by an institution with learners under 18 (e.g. Synvord School Management), the institution must obtain verifiable parental consent as required by the DPDP Act.

2. Legal basis

We process personal data with your consent (which you give by sending an enquiry, creating an account or accepting analytics), or for legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act: to provide a service you requested, to comply with law (e.g. tax records), or to respond to a legal claim. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 6). Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.

3. How we use it

To respond to you; provide, support, secure and improve our products; send invoices and service messages; meet legal obligations; and, only if you agree, send occasional product news (you can unsubscribe in any email). We do not sell personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run SSPART and who are bound by contract to protect it: website hosting (GitHub Pages), our database provider for enquiries and product data (e.g. Supabase), email hosting, payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay), cloud hosting (AWS / Azure), analytics (Google Analytics 4, only with your consent), and our professional advisers (accountant, lawyer). We may disclose data if required by law, a court or a government authority. Some providers store data outside India; we use providers with appropriate safeguards and follow any restrictions notified by the Government of India under the DPDP Act.

5. How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to 24 months after our last contact. Account and product data: for as long as your account is active, then deleted or anonymised within 90 days unless a contract says otherwise. Invoices and payment records: 8 years, as required by Indian tax law. Job applications: 12 months. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, where we no longer need it for the purpose or by law;
  • withdraw consent you gave earlier;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • have your grievance addressed (see our Grievance Officer page).

Email grievance@sspart-enterprise.com from the address we hold, or write to us at the address below. We will acknowledge within 24 hours and respond within 15 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest, role-based access with two-factor authentication, least-privilege access for staff and contractors, logging, and regular backups. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform affected people and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires, and report cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours.

8. Cookies

The website sets no cookies unless you accept analytics. See our Cookies policy.

9. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the "last updated" date. For significant changes affecting product users, we will also notify you by email or in the product.
