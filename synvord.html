Built into every module

The platform underneath does the hard parts once, for everything you use.

  • Your data stays yoursEach business is walled off from every other customer by design, and tested on every release.
  • Consent built inNo message is sent and no data is used without a recorded consent; a withdrawal applies within an hour.
  • Choose your data regionYour account stays in one region: India, Singapore, the EU or the Americas.
  • Your brand, not oursYour logo, colours, fonts, domain and sender identities. Guests never see Synvord unless you want them to.
  • AI with human approvalAI drafts, recommends and flags. Nothing is sent or changed until a named person approves it.
  • Encrypted personal dataPersonal data is encrypted, and every read is logged for you to review.
  • Flexible sign-inStaff use an authenticator code or Google or Microsoft single sign-on; customers use a phone or email code.
  • Documents and e-signingTax-ready invoices with gap-free numbering, multilingual PDFs and click-to-sign with an audit trail.
  • Global from day oneMultiple currencies, time zones and languages, with tax and statutory rules per country.

Questions

When will Synvord be available?

Synvord is being built now, and modules open one at a time. Ask for early access and we will tell you when the ones you need are ready.

Do I have to buy everything?

No. Start with the module you need and add others later. They share one customer record, so nothing has to be entered twice.

How will it be priced?

Modules your staff work in, such as Stay and Table, are priced per user. Modules that send messages, such as Reach and Connect, are priced by how many messages you send. We will share prices with early-access customers.

Can it carry our own brand?

Yes. Every business and every property can have its own logo, colours, domain and sender identity. Your guests and customers see your brand, not ours.

Where is our data stored?

In one region you choose: India, Singapore, the EU or the Americas. Your data is never shared with another business on Synvord.

Will AI act on its own?

No. AI drafts, recommends and explains. A person on your team approves every message, price change and automated action.
