Questions

Is Synvord the same as Neel?

Yes. Neel is now called Synvord. It is the same product, and existing installations carry on as before.

How is Synvord priced?

We quote for each business, based on the number of stores and franchises and the setup work needed. Book a demo and we will share a quote.

Does Synvord handle GST?

Yes. Bills carry HSN codes and CGST and SGST, or IGST, and Synvord prepares the GSTR-1 registers and a GSTR-3B view. You or your accountant file the returns. Government e-invoices and e-way bills are not supported yet.

How do franchises work?

Each franchise is a separately owned, GST-registered store with its own stock and books. Head office supplies it through supply orders, and dispatch raises the invoice and drafts the matching purchase bill in the franchise's books.

Which payment methods does the counter accept?

Cash, UPI, card, credit note, or a split of these. Customers can also buy on credit and pay later, and bills can carry a UPI link and QR.

Does it run payroll?

Yes: attendance, leave, salary advances, payroll runs and payslips. Statutory deductions such as PF, ESI and TDS are not calculated yet, so handle those with your accountant.

Where does our data live?

On your own server, or on a server we host for you. For installations we host, we run backups as agreed in your service terms.
