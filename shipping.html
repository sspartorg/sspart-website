Shipping and delivery policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

SSPART Enterprise Private Limited sells software and services only. We do not ship physical goods.

1. How we deliver

What you buy How it is delivered When
Product subscription (Atlas Team, DheQuest, Synvord, Experts Jar) Account activation or licence details sent to your registered email Within 24 hours of payment confirmation, usually immediately
Atlas Enterprise / product setup Installation in your systems or cloud, as agreed in the proposal On the dates in the proposal
Custom software and services Source code in your repository, deployments, documentation and reports On the milestone dates in your proposal or Statement of Work
Training sessions Online meeting link sent by email On the agreed date

2. Delivery confirmation

You will receive an email confirming activation or delivery. If you have not received access within 24 hours of a successful payment, email support@sspart-enterprise.com with your transaction number and we will resolve it the same working day.

3. Charges

There are no shipping or delivery charges. Any third-party costs (e.g. your own cloud hosting) are set out in the proposal.

4. Service area

We deliver to customers in India and internationally, subject to applicable law.
