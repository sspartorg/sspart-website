Long (200 words)

SSPART Enterprise Private Limited is a product engineering company based in Hyderabad, India. Founded in 2019 as an engineering services firm delivering web and mobile software, SSPART now builds and runs its own product lines alongside custom software for businesses and enterprise teams.

At the centre is Atlas, SSPART's agent orchestration platform. Atlas turns each software request into small, testable pieces of work, runs specialised AI agents on them in parallel, and checks every result automatically before an engineer reviews and signs off the delivery. Teams can use Atlas on the AI subscription they already pay for: it is free to start, with a Team plan for multi-agent workflows.

SSPART's other products serve specific industries. DheQuest takes trading strategies from idea to back-test to live automation for active traders. Neel runs the storefront, counter and back office for retailers and every franchise. Experts Jar builds job-ready skills through real assignments reviewed one-on-one by industry experts.

SSPART's clients include brokerages and payments companies. It works cloud-native on AWS and Azure, and custom work belongs to the client: source code, data and intellectual property stay in the client's own repositories and cloud.
