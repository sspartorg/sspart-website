Cookies policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

The SSPART website sets no cookies by default. It asks for your permission before using Google Analytics, and it sets analytics cookies only if you click "Accept".

What we use

Cookie / storage Set by Purpose When Duration
sspart-analytics-consent (browser local storage, not a cookie) sspart-enterprise.com Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics, so we don't ask again When you click Accept or Decline Until you clear your browser data
_ga, _ga_<ID> Google Analytics 4 (google.com) Counts visits and shows which pages are useful, with IP anonymisation Only after you click Accept Up to 14 months
Supabase session (browser local storage, not a cookie) sspart-enterprise.com Keeps SSPART staff signed in to the private staff page; visitors never use it Only when a staff member signs in Until sign-out

Our icons load from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and our fonts are served from our own site; neither sets cookies.

Changing your choice

To change your choice at any time, open Cookie settings from the link at the bottom of any page. Choosing "Essential only" stops Google Analytics and deletes its cookies. You can also block cookies in your browser or install Google's opt-out add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

Product cookies

When you sign in to an SSPART product, the product uses strictly necessary cookies or tokens to keep you signed in and secure. These are essential to provide the service and are described in the product.

Questions about cookies: grievance@sspart-enterprise.com. See also our Privacy policy.
