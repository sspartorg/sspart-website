Questions

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The kitchen display and staff ordering keep running on the local network for at least 4 hours, then sync when you're back online.

Do diners need an app?

No. They scan the table QR code and order in the browser. A phone code is optional and links the order to their record.

Can I use it without a hotel?

Yes. Standalone outlets run on their own; the PMS is only needed for charging to a room.

Which kitchen hardware do I need?

Any Android tablet works as a kitchen display, and standard receipt printers work alongside it.

Do you deliver orders for us?

No. Aggregator orders come into your queue, and your own riders can be assigned to your own deliveries.
