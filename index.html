    Questions clients ask us

    Anything else, ask us directly.

    How does Atlas make delivery faster and cheaper?

    Atlas breaks each request into small pieces of work, runs specialised agents on them in parallel and matches the AI effort to each task, so nothing is overspent. Every result is checked and reviewed before an engineer signs off the delivery, and the cost of each delivery is tracked so you see what the spend achieved.

    Does Atlas need access to our cloud?

    No. Atlas delivers into your repositories, where your team reviews every change. When we build for you, Atlas runs on our systems and the result is deployed to your own AWS or Azure account. If you license Atlas, it runs on your own systems.

    Do you only sell your own products?

    No. We license and customise our products, and we also build custom software from scratch. Many engagements combine both: a product as the base, with custom work on top.

    Can a product be branded and customised for us?

    Yes. Synvord will carry your own logo, colours, domain and sender identity, and every product can be extended with your own workflows, reports and integrations.

    Where is our data hosted?

    It depends on the product. Custom work runs in your own AWS or Azure account; Atlas can be self-hosted; DheQuest is a hosted service; Synvord will keep your data in the region you choose. We can also host and operate custom work for you.

    Who owns the code for custom work?

    You do. Source code, data and documentation from custom engagements are yours and live in your repositories.

    What happens after go-live?

    We offer support plans with monitoring, fixes and planned improvements, or a full handover to your team with documentation and training.

    How is an engagement priced?

    Fixed scope and fixed price for well-defined work, or a dedicated monthly team for products that keep evolving. Atlas pricing is published here. For our other products, pricing depends on how widely you use them, and we share it on a demo call.
