Everything a hospital runs on

For hospital administrators, medical superintendents, nursing directors and care-home managers.

  • Registration and patient indexOne record per patient with a permanent MRN, duplicate checks and barcoded wristbands.
  • Appointments and queuesBook from the counter, call centre, web, WhatsApp or partners, then manage tokens, waits and teleconsults.
  • Emergency and triageFast arrival registration, five-level triage with time targets, a live emergency board and a mass-casualty mode.
  • Admissions and bed boardA live bed board for admissions, transfers and discharges, where no bed is ever allocated twice.
  • Operating theatresSchedule cases for privileged surgeons only, run the WHO checklist, scan implants and track theatre use.
  • Pharmacy dispensingDispense against signed orders with a wristband scan; expired or recalled batches are blocked.
  • Lab and radiology ordersRoute orders, print barcoded sample labels, track samples to the lab and record critical-value calls.
  • Billing and insuranceReal-time charges, packages, deposits, estimates, pre-authorisation timers and a claims workspace for each payer.
  • Care homes and familyAssessments, care plans, medication rounds and falls response, plus family updates limited to what the patient allows.

AI that drafts. People decide.

AI proposes. A qualified person decides. Patient data stays in your health-data region.

  • Discharge forecastingShows expected discharges per ward over the next 24 and 72 hours, with reasons. It never changes a discharge date.
  • Claim checkingChecks claim packs for missing documents and mismatched codes, and drafts replies to payer queries for staff to edit.
  • Handover draftsDrafts each patient's shift handover, labelled as AI-drafted until the nurse edits and signs it.
  • No-show predictionSuggests reminders or overbooking from booking patterns only; clinical data is not used.

Questions

Does it work for care homes?

Yes. A care-home profile runs on the same system, with assessments, care plans, medication rounds and falls response.

Can families see patient information?

Only what the patient or their legal guardian has allowed, purpose by purpose. Condition updates are non-diagnostic.

Where is patient data stored?

In the health-data region for your country: in-country for India, UAE and Singapore, and a US region for US providers.

Is the medical record included?

Clinical notes, results and discharge summaries come with Synvord EMR, a separate module. Without it, HMS runs in an administrative mode with no clinical data.

What isn't included?

Blood bank and home-health visits come later. Imaging storage (PACS), ambulance dispatch and clinician payroll are not part of HMS.

When will Synvord HMS be available?

It is being built now. Ask for early access and we will tell you as soon as it opens.
