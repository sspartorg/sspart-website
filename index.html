How these numbers are worked out

A mix of measured and estimated values, not a quote. The Atlas AI cost and turnaround are measured: across 12 representative requests run end to end in our internal benchmark, each was delivered in 17 to 78 minutes of run time, at an average of $11.75. Engineer time, the AI spend of people iterating on their own and the rework rate are our estimates from typical projects. Every option is priced at a blended engineer rate of $400 a day. Turnaround is working days from request to delivery for one change.

  • Manual team: 3 engineer-days per change, covering build, testing and review; about 5 working days turnaround once hand-offs and queues are included.
  • People iterating with AI: 1.5 engineer-days and about $25 of AI use per change, with 30% of changes needing rework; about 3 working days turnaround.
  • With Atlas: half an engineer-day per change to steer and sign off, plus $11.75 of AI, the average cost per delivery in our internal benchmark; delivered within a working day.

Questions clients ask us

Anything else, ask us directly.

How does Atlas make delivery faster and cheaper?

Atlas breaks each request into small pieces of work, runs specialised agents on them in parallel and matches the AI effort to each task, so nothing is overspent. Every result is checked and reviewed before an engineer signs off the delivery, and the cost of each delivery is tracked so you see what the spend achieved.

Does Atlas need access to our cloud?

No. Atlas runs on our own machines and delivers into your repositories, where your team can review every change. What we build is deployed to your own AWS or Azure account.

Do you only sell your own products?

No. We license and customise our products, and we also build custom software from scratch. Many engagements combine both: a product as the base, with custom work on top.

Can a product be branded and customised for us?

Yes. Neel ships with four themes and a store profile you control, and every product can be extended with your own workflows, reports and integrations.

Where is our data hosted?

It depends on the product. Custom work runs in your own AWS or Azure account; Atlas and Neel can be self-hosted; DheQuest is a hosted service. We can also host and operate custom work for you.

Who owns the code for custom work?

You do. Source code, data and documentation from custom engagements are yours and live in your repositories.

What happens after go-live?

We offer support plans with monitoring, fixes and planned improvements, or a full handover to your team with documentation and training.

How is an engagement priced?

Fixed scope and fixed price for well-defined work, or a dedicated monthly team for products that keep evolving. Product pricing depends on the product and how widely you use it, and we share it on a demo call.
